15.2. Definitions🔗

The following custom definitions and abbreviations are introduced in this guide:

Chapter 2: SimpleGraph🔗

  • pathNat: path graph on ℕ defined using the default SimpleGraph constructor.

  • pathNatFromRel: path graph on ℕ defined using SimpleGraph.fromRel.

  • pathNatFromEdgeSet: path graph on ℕ defined using SimpleGraph.fromEdgeSet.

From the exercises:

  • shiftedPathNat: graph on ℕ with an edge from n to n + 1 for every n ≥ i.

  • shortPathNat: graph on ℕ with an edge from n to n + 1 for every n < i.

Chapter 3: Constructions🔗

  • paleyGraph: Paley graph of a field F.

  • cayleyS4: Cayley graph of the symmetric group on Fin 4 generated by a 3-cycle and a 4-cycle.

  • SimpleGraph.sigma / Σg: disjoint union of an indexed family of graphs.

  • sigmaCompleteNat: disjoint union of complete graphs K[n].

  • hypercubeHasse: Hasse diagram construction of the n-dimensional hypercube.

  • hypercubeHamming: Hamming cube definition of the n-dimensional hypercube.

  • HypercubeVert: recursive definition of the vertex set of the hypercube.

  • hypercube / Q[n]: recursive box product definition of the n-dimensional hypercube.

  • mycielskian / mμ(G): the Mycielskian of a graph G.

  • grotzschGraph: Grötzsch graph (i.e. mμ(C[5])).

  • mycielskian.left / vˡ: left copy of a vertex in the Mycielskian.

  • mycielskian.right / vʳ: right copy of a vertex in the Mycielskian.

  • MycielskianVert / mμᵥ^n(V): vertex type of the n-th iterated Mycielskian.

  • iterMycielskian / mμ^n(G): n-th iterated Mycielskian of G.

  • completeMultipartiteFinNat: complete multipartite graph with copies of ℕ as classes.

  • completeMultipartiteNatFin: complete multipartite graph with classes Fin n.

  • blowUpGraph: blow-up of a graph G by replacing each vertex with a class of vertices.

  • blowUpN / G[n]: n-uniform blow-up of a graph G.

  • kneserGraph / Kn[n, k]: the Kneser graph — vertices are the k-element subsets of β (or of Fin n), with edges joining distinct disjoint subsets.

  • petersenGraph: the Petersen graph, i.e. Kn[5, 2].

Chapter 4: Decidability, Finiteness and Degrees🔗

  • myIfThenElse: branches with bif on the Boolean decide p from a Decidable p instance to return a string, mirroring how ite is defined.

  • myClassicalIfThenElse: the same function built with classical, hence noncomputable.

  • pathNatFromRelAbbrev: pathNatFromRel restated as an abbrev, so that Lean can synthesize DecidableRel for its adjacency.

  • locallyFiniteOfFintypeEdgeSet: a graph with finitely many edges is locally finite.

  • blowUpNFintypeNeighborSet: a vertex ⟨v, i⟩ of the blow-up G[n] has a finite neighbourhood when v has one in G (a def, not an instance).

  • degIn: the number of neighbours of v lying in a finite set s, defined as the size of Mathlib's s.bipartiteAbove G.Adj v.

  • hypercubeVertToBin: conversion of a hypercube vertex to its binary string representation.

  • hypercubeFintypeNeighborSet: an efficient Fintype instance for the neighbourhoods of Q[n], built through the box product (a def, not an instance).

  • degreeMultiset: the multiset of degrees of all vertices in a finite graph.

  • degreeSequence: the list of degrees of all vertices in a finite graph, sorted in non-increasing order.

  • Graphic: predicate for a multiset being the degree multiset of some finite simple graph.

  • ErdosGallaiLE: the k-th Erdős–Gallai inequality for a multiset of degrees.

  • havelHakimiAux: the Havel–Hakimi test on a non-increasing list, merging rather than re-sorting between rounds.

  • havelHakimi: decides whether a multiset of degrees is graphic via Havel–Hakimi.

  • coneGraph: adjoins a new vertex to a graph, adjacent to a chosen set of vertices.

  • havelHakimiEdges: constructive Havel–Hakimi, returning the edge list of a graph realizing a labelled graphic degree sequence.

  • graphOfEdgeList: the graph on Fin n with a given list of edges, used to check the output of havelHakimiEdges.

From the exercises:

  • blowUpGraphFintypeNeighborSet: a vertex w of a blow-up has a finite neighbourhood if w.fst does and the classes over its neighbours are finite.

Chapter 5: Maps🔗

  • pathNat.translate: embedding of the infinite path pathNat into itself.

  • Embedding.blowUp: canonical embedding of a graph G into its blow-up.

  • Iso.emptyGraph: the isomorphism emptyGraph α ≃g emptyGraph β induced by an equivalence α ≃ β.

  • hypercube.embedBool / hypercube.embedFalse / hypercube.embedTrue: embeddings of Q[n] into Q[n + 1].

  • Embedding.mycielskianLeft: embedding of a graph G into its Mycielskian mμ(G).

  • Hom.mapMycielskian: graph homomorphism mμ(G) →g mμ(H) induced by a homomorphism G →g H.

  • Embedding.mapMycielskian: graph embedding mμ(G) ↪g mμ(H) induced by an embedding G ↪g H.

  • Iso.mapMycielskian: graph isomorphism mμ(G) ≃g mμ(H) induced by an isomorphism G ≃g H.

  • IsVertexTransitive: predicate for a graph being vertex-transitive.

  • cycleGraph.rotate: automorphism of C[n] rotating the vertices modulo n, sending d to 0.

  • HypercubeVert.xor / ⊕ₕ: bitwise xor addition on hypercube vertices.

  • hypercube.translate: automorphism of Q[n] by translating by a vertex.

  • mycielskianCompleteTwoIsoCycleFive: the isomorphism mμ(K[2]) ≃g C[5].

  • tensorProd / G ⊗g H: tensor (categorical) product of two graphs, on the vertex type V × W.

  • tensorProdFintypeNeighborSet: finite neighbourhoods in G ⊗g H from finite neighbourhoods in G and H (a def, not an instance).

  • tensorProd.fstHom / tensorProd.sndHom: the two projections out of the tensor product as graph homomorphisms.

  • tensorProd.prod: the homomorphism into tensorProd G H induced by a pair of homomorphisms (universal property of the product).

  • piTensorProd / ⨂g i, G i: tensor product of an arbitrary family of graphs.

  • piTensorProdFintypeNeighborSet: finite neighbourhoods in ⨂g i, G i from finite neighbourhoods in each G i, for a finite nonempty index type (a def, not an instance).

  • IndFree: A.IndFree B means that B contains no induced copy of A.

  • ClawFree: a graph is claw-free if it contains no induced copy of the claw K[1, 3].

  • lineGraphCompleteThreeIsoCompleteThree: the isomorphism L[K[3]] ≃g K[3].

  • lineGraphClawIsoCompleteThree: the isomorphism L[K[1, 3]] ≃g K[3].

From the exercises:

  • pathGraph.embedding: embedding of a path graph P[n] into a longer path P[n + k].

  • Embedding.mapBlowUp: embedding of G into the blow-up H.blowUpGraph U given G ↪g H and nonempty parts.

  • hypercube.embedding: embedding of Q[n] into Q[n + k] by iterating hypercube.embedFalse.

  • hypercubeTwoIsoCycleFour: the Gray-code isomorphism Q[2] ≃g C[4].

Chapter 6: Cliques🔗

  • mycielskianProj: projection from the vertex set of mμ(G) to G mapping both left and right copies to the original vertex.

  • openNeighborhoodSet: the open neighbourhood of a set s, i.e. the union of the neighbourhoods of its vertices.

  • openNeighborhoodFinset: the Finset version, for a locally finite graph: the vertices adjacent to at least one vertex of s.

Chapter 7: Colorings🔗

  • completeBipartiteGraph.bicoloring: the natural Bool-colouring of completeBipartiteGraph V W (true on the right part); a correctly named alias of Mathlib's CompleteBipartiteGraph.bicoloring.

  • pathNat.bicoloring: the natural Bool-colouring of the infinite path pathNat by parity.

  • Coloring.ofBoxProdLeft / Coloring.ofBoxProdRight: the colourings of G and H obtained by restricting a colouring of G □ H along a fixed vertex of the other factor.

  • Coloring.boxProd: the colouring (v, w) ↦ C₁ v + C₂ w of G □ H built from colourings of G and H, for colours with a cancellative addition (IsCancelAdd), such as Fin k (addition modulo k).

  • hypercube.bicoloring: the natural Bool-colouring of the hypercube Q[n], built by induction on n with Coloring.boxProd.

  • HypercubeVert.parity: the parity (Bool.xor of the coordinates) of a hypercube vertex, which is its colour under hypercube.bicoloring.

  • Coloring.mycielskianLast: the equivalent (k + 1)-colouring of mμ(G) in which the vertex none receives colour k.

  • Coloring.ofMycielskian: the k-colouring of G induced by a (k + 1)-colouring of mμ(G).

  • Coloring.mycielskian: the (k + 1)-colouring of mμ(G) induced by a k-colouring of G.

  • neighborLTSet: set of neighbours of a vertex v that are smaller than v.

  • neighborLTFinset: finset of neighbours of a vertex v that are smaller than v.

  • degreeLT: the number of earlier neighbours of a vertex v.

  • LocallyLTFinite: every vertex has finitely many earlier neighbours (the LT analogue of LocallyFinite).

  • greedyChoice: the least colour not used by any earlier neighbour of a vertex, via Nat.find.

  • greedy: the greedy ℕ-colouring of a graph on ℕ, defined by well-founded recursion.

  • greedyColoringNat: greedy bundled as a G.Coloring ℕ for G : SimpleGraph ℕ.

  • crownGraph: the complete bipartite graph on the even and odd naturals minus the perfect matching 2 * n ↦ 2 * n + 1.

  • crownGraph.bicoloring: the natural Bool-colouring of crownGraph by parity.

  • encodeMap: a graph on an encodable vertex type transported to ℕ via SimpleGraph.map; its adjacency involves existential quantifiers.

  • SimpleGraph.encode: a graph on an encodable vertex type transported to a graph on ℕ via Encodable.decode₂, preserving decidability of adjacency.

  • Embedding.encode: the graph embedding of G into G.encode.

  • greedyColoringFin: the greedy Fin (Δ + 1)-colouring of a locally finite graph with all degrees at most Δ on an encodable vertex type.

  • greedyTraced: a copy of greedy that dbg_traces a * on every call, used to count recursive calls.

  • withTraceCount: runs a computation with its output captured, returning the value together with the number of trace lines.

  • greedyChoiceMin: the greedy choice computed as a Finset.min' over the finitely many candidate colours {0, ..., k}, where k is the number of colours used by earlier neighbours.

  • greedyList: the list of the first n greedy colours, built by structural recursion so that each colour is computed once.

  • greedyMemo: the memoized greedy colouring, reading the last entry of greedyList; greedy is compiled to it via @[csimp].

  • greedyColoring: the greedy ℕ-colouring of a graph on an encodable vertex type, colouring each vertex by the greedy colour of its encoding.

From the exercises:

  • kneserGraph.coloring: the explicit (n - 2 * k + 2)-colouring of the Kneser graph Kn[n, k] by least element.

  • starNat: the infinite star on ℕ, in which 0 is adjacent to every other vertex.

Chapter 8: Walks🔗

  • pathNat.path: walk on the infinite path from i to i + j.

  • pathGraph.path: walk along the finite path graph P[n + 1] from 0 to Fin.last n.

  • HypercubeVert.hammingPath: path between two hypercube vertices.

  • HypercubeVert.hammingDist: Hamming distance between two hypercube vertices (number of differing coordinates).

  • HypercubeVert.grayStart / HypercubeVert.grayEnd: start and end vertices of the Gray code walk.

  • hypercube.grayPath: Gray code walk on Q[n].

  • Walk.mycielskianZigZag: zig-zag walk in mμ(G).

  • Walk.mycielskian: walk in mμ(G) constructed from a walk in G (protected, to coexist with the graph constructor mycielskian).

  • Walk.mycielskianClosed: closed walk in mμ(G) constructed from a closed walk in G (a cycle if the original is one).

  • Walk.posaRotate: Pósa rotation of a path walk.

  • Walk.posaClosed: closing a Pósa path to obtain a cycle.

  • Walk.IsMaximalPath: a maximal path — a path that cannot be extended at either end, i.e. every neighbour of either endpoint already lies on it.

  • IsOrePair: Ore's condition at a pair of vertices — if distinct and non-adjacent, their degree sum is at least |V|.

  • IsClosed: a closed graph — one with no eligible pair, i.e. any two distinct vertices with degree sum at least |V| are adjacent.

  • closureStep: one round of the closure process — join every eligible pair at once.

  • closureAux: fuel-limited iteration of closureStep.

  • closure: the Bondy–Chvátal closure of a graph (computable).

  • IsOreGraph: an Ore graph — every pair of vertices satisfies Ore's condition IsOrePair.

From the exercises:

  • hypercube.grayCycle: Gray code Hamiltonian cycle in Q[n + 2].

  • chordedC5: C[5] with the two chords 0 — 2 and 0 — 3, a graph that is not an Ore graph but whose closure is complete.

  • Walk.IsMaximumPath: a maximum-length path — a path such that no path in G is longer.

Chapter 9: Subgraphs🔗

  • Subgraph.mycielskianLeft: lift of a subgraph in G to a subgraph in mμ(G).

  • matchingNum / ν G: matching number of G (in ℕ∞, mirroring SimpleGraph.vertexCoverNum), the maximum number of edges in a matching of G.

  • Subgraph.boxProdLeft: a subgraph M of G copied into every G-fibre of G □ H.

  • hypercube.matching: perfect matching of Q[n + 1], defined recursively as ⊤ on Q[1] and the fibre-copy of the previous matching thereafter.

  • Subgraph.mycielskianDouble: the subgraph of mμ(G) with edges xˡ — yʳ and xʳ — yˡ for each edge x — y of a subgraph of G.

  • Subgraph.mycielskianDoubleDartEmbedding: embedding of the darts of a subgraph into the edge set of its Mycielskian double.

  • ColoringOn: colouring of every vertex of G that need only be proper on the edges of G with both ends in s.

  • ColorableOn: predicate for the existence of a G.ColoringOn (Fin n) s.

  • ColoringOn.restrict: restriction of a G.ColoringOn γ s to any t ⊆ s.

  • ColoringOn.copy: transport a G.ColoringOn γ s along a proof that s = t.

  • ColoringOn.toColoring: a G.ColoringOn γ Set.univ is a G.Coloring γ.

  • Coloring.toColoringOn: the ColoringOn of a set s induced by a colouring of the whole graph.

  • ColoringOn.empty: the colouring of the empty set of vertices by a given colour c.

  • ColoringOn.singleton: colouring of {a} by any colour c.

  • ColoringOn.ofNotAdj: colouring of {a, b} by a single colour c when ¬ G.Adj a b.

  • ColoringOn.union: combination of compatible colourings of s and t into a colouring of s ∪ t.

  • ColoringOn.insert: extension of a colouring of s to insert a s using a colour not used on neighbours of a in s.

  • ColoringOn.map: relabelling of the colours of a partial colouring along an injective map.

Chapter 10: Connectivity🔗

  • isoSigmaConnectedComponents: the isomorphism between G and the sigma graph of its connected components.

  • EdgesInOneComponent: every edge of G lies in a single connected component (any two non-isolated vertices are reachable from each other).

  • locallyFiniteDeleteEdges: the graph G.deleteEdges s obtained by deleting edges from a locally finite graph is locally finite.

  • IsVertexConnected: predicate for a graph being k-vertex-connected (k : ℕ∞, as for Mathlib's IsEdgeConnected).

  • edgeConnectivity / λ(G): the edge connectivity λ(G) : ℕ∞, the supremum of the k for which G is k-edge-connected.

  • vertexConnectivity / κ(G): the vertex connectivity κ(G) : ℕ∞, the supremum of the k for which G is k-vertex-connected.

  • walkComponent: the set of vertices of a finite set W reachable from v₂ by a walk lying inside W.

  • IsCutVertex: x is a cut vertex of G (some two vertices other than x are joined by a walk, but every walk between them passes through x).

  • CycleEquivalent: two edges are cycle-equivalent if they are equal or lie on a common cycle.

  • BlockEquivalent: the equivalence relation on edges generated by CycleEquivalent.

  • blockSetoid: the setoid instance on G.edgeSet given by block-equivalence.

  • Block: a block of G, i.e. a block-equivalence class of edges.

  • blockMk: the block containing a given edge.

  • Adj.block: the block containing the edge u — v, written h.block for h : G.Adj u v.

  • Block.toSubgraph: the subgraph of G underlying a block, formed by the edges of G in that block and their endpoints.

  • Block.map: the image of a block under a copy f : Copy G G', the block containing the images of its edges.

  • Subgraph.homOfMapLE: a homomorphism f restricts to a homomorphism H.coe →g H'.coe whenever H.map f ≤ H'.

  • SplitsAt: G is the union of two proper subgraphs whose supports meet in at most a given vertex.

From the exercises:

  • botComponentEquiv: equivalence between the connected components of the empty graph and its vertex type.

  • Hom.sigmaDesc: the universal property of the disjoint union: a family of homomorphisms G i →g H gives a homomorphism Σg G →g H.

  • Finset.fiberProj: the vertices z of G whose copy (z, b) in the G-fibre at b of G □ K₂ lies in a given vertex set.

  • bowtie: two triangles sharing a vertex, a graph with κ(G) < λ(G).

  • barbell: two copies of K[4] joined by an edge, a graph with λ(G) < δ(G).

Chapter 11: Distance🔗

  • EdgesFormMatching: the edges form a matching, i.e. every vertex has at most one neighbour.

  • mycielskianFourCycle: 4-cycle in mμ(G) using none and left/right copies.

  • layer: the i-th distance layer around a vertex r, i.e. the Finset of vertices at distance exactly i from r.

Chapter 12: Algebraic Graph Theory🔗

  • Iso.autCongr: transport of the automorphism group of a graph along an isomorphism.

  • cycleGraph.reflect: automorphism of C[n] reflecting the vertices (k ↦ -k).

  • autCycleGraphMulEquivDihedral: the automorphism group of C[n + 3] is isomorphic to DihedralGroup (n + 3).

  • autHypercubeTwoMulEquivDihedral: the automorphism group of Q[2] is isomorphic to DihedralGroup 4.

  • mycielskianLift: the vector on the vertices of mμ(G) with value 0 at the apex, t * x v at vˡ and x v at vʳ.

  • HypercubeVert.equivPi: equivalence between the hypercube vertex types HypercubeVert n and Fin n → Bool, via Fin.snoc.

  • hypercubeIsoHamming: the hypercube Q[n] is isomorphic to the Hamming cube hypercubeHamming n.

  • hypercubeHamming.translate / hypercubeHamming.permute: translation by a fixed vector (pointwise xor) and coordinate permutation as automorphisms of the Hamming cube.

  • hyperoctahedralAction: the action of Equiv.Perm (Fin n) on translation vectors by permuting coordinates.

  • HyperoctahedralGroup: the hyperoctahedral group (ℤ/2)ⁿ ⋊ Sₙ, i.e. the wreath product ℤ/2 ≀ Sₙ, as a semidirect product.

  • hypercubeHamming.translateHom / hypercubeHamming.permuteHom: translations and coordinate permutations as monoid homs into the automorphism group of the Hamming cube.

  • hyperoctahedralToAut: the homomorphism from the hyperoctahedral group to the automorphisms of the Hamming cube.

  • autHypercubeHammingMulEquivHyperoctahedral: the automorphism group of the Hamming cube is the hyperoctahedral group.

  • autHypercubeMulEquivHyperoctahedral: the automorphism group of Q[n] is the hyperoctahedral group ℤ/2 ≀ Sₙ.

  • chosenSubgraphIso: a choice, for each subgraph C of B isomorphic to A, of a reference isomorphism A ≃g C.coe.

  • subgraphIsoEquivAut: equivalence between the isomorphisms A ≃g C.coe of a subgraph C isomorphic to A and the automorphisms of A.

  • copyEquivSigmaIso: equivalence between A.Copy B and the Sigma type of subgraphs of B isomorphic to A with their isomorphisms.

  • copyEquivSubgraphIsoProdAut: equivalence A.Copy B ≃ {C : B.Subgraph | Nonempty (A ≃g C.coe)} × (A ≃g A).

  • Iso.copyEquivIso: for finite A, an isomorphism A ≃g B identifies copies of A in B with isomorphisms A ≃g B.

  • copyEquivAut: copies of a finite graph in itself are equivalent to its automorphisms.

  • copyEquivSubgraphIsoProdCopy: A.Copy B is equivalent to the subgraphs of B isomorphic to A paired with copies of A in itself.

  • Iso.copyCongr: isomorphisms A ≃g A' and B ≃g B' identify the copies of A in B with the copies of A' in B'.

  • extendEmb / hypercubeHamming.extendHom: extension of a k-vector to an n-vector along an embedding of directions Fin k ↪ Fin n, and the induced homomorphism of Hamming cubes.

  • subcubeCopy: the canonical copy of Q[k] in Q[n] with a given base point and set of free directions.

  • hypercubeHamming.copyEquivProd: the copies of Q[k] in Q[n] correspond to pairs of a base point and a direction embedding.

  • copyEquivEmbedding: if every copy of A in B reflects adjacency, then copies of A in B are the same as embeddings A ↪g B.

From the exercises:

  • autMulLeftCayley: left multiplication by g as an automorphism of the Cayley graph mulCayley s.

  • autTopMulEquivPerm: the automorphism group of a complete graph is the symmetric group.

  • autComplMulEquiv: a graph and its complement have the same automorphism group.

  • boolVecEquivFinset: Bool vectors of length n correspond to subsets of Fin n.

  • hypercubeHammingIsoHasse: the Hamming cube is isomorphic to the Hasse diagram hypercubeHasse n.

  • copyTopEquiv: copies of a graph in a complete graph are the injective maps of vertices.

Chapter 13: Theorems🔗

  • IsCompleteMultipartite.finpartition: the parts of a finite complete multipartite graph, as a Finpartition of its vertices.

  • IsCopyExtremal: G has property p and the most copies of H among the graphs on its vertices with property p, generalizing Mathlib's IsExtremal from edges.

  • IsNCliqueMaximal: G is s-clique-maximal, that is, (r + 1)-clique-free and with the most s-cliques among such graphs on its vertices.

  • cliqueUnion: the union of the s-cliques of G; distinct vertices are adjacent when they lie in a common s-clique.

  • IsDirection: a direction of G, a consistent choice of a component of G - K for every finite set K of vertices (the component for L lies inside the one for K whenever K ⊆ L).
